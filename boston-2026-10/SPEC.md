## 1. The page — two questions, with the grid behind them

A single static page, phone-first (it is opened from a QR code in a gym lobby on weak wifi): **no
framework, no runtime fetch, no third-party script, no web font request** — system fonts, inline CSS, one
small inline script. Total transfer under 60 KB before images.

1. **Top-level tab: Individual · Family** (Family is its own section — *"really it's '3 + 1'"*).
2. **Individual, question 1 — "What's your goal?"** → Lean · Signature · Performance, each with its
   one-line promise and calorie/protein range from the plan table.
3. **Individual, question 2 — "Which meals should we cover?"** → *Lunch or dinner* (7 a week) · *Lunch and
   dinner* (14 a week). ⭐ The counts are data (`data/plans.json`), so 5/10 or a third option (breakfast,
   21) is a data change, not a code change.
4. **The result**: the chosen plan, meals per week, **price per meal and the weekly total**, and one button:
   **"Choose your meals"** → the plan's order page (§ 3).
5. ⭐ **"See all plans"** reveals the full **3 × 2 grid** (size × count) with the same price and total per
   cell — the fallback for anyone who would rather pick directly, and the demo's *"grid of 3 x 2 links"*.
6. **Family**: its one plan, its price, and the same button.
7. ⭐ **The choice lives in the URL fragment** (`#lean-7`, `#signature-14`, `#family`), so a link — and later
   a saved offer — **reopens the exact choice**. Back and forward work.
8. Branding: Fit AF's public palette — navy `#1b2360`, orange `#ff931e`, ink `#212529`, canvas
   `#f9f9fb`, teal accent `#64d2d3` — and the public logo if one is committed under `assets/` (else the
   wordmark "FIT AF" in text). ⛔ No offer, discount or contact capture in rung 1: those need the
   Owner's offer and consent wording (later rungs).

> **Amended 2026-09-27 (branding pass, branch `boston/branding`).** "No web font request" now means *no
> request to another host*: Poppins (600/700) and Open Sans (400/600) are **self-hosted** Latin WOFF2
> subsets under `src/fonts/` (OFL, `font-display: swap`, ~53 KB), and the store's logo is served from
> `src/assets/`. Both load from the page's own origin; T5 checks that every `url()` and `<img src>` is
> same-origin or `data:`. The palette gains the store's plan colours and a darker button orange,
> checked by `npm run contrast` (APCA) — see the README.

Read 2026-09-27 off `https://fitafnutrition.com/meal-plans` (the store's public page):

| plan | 4 | 7 | 10 | 14 | 21 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lean | 20 · $13.00 | 21 · $12.50 | 22 · $12.25 | 23 · $12.00 | 24 · $11.50 |

| Performance | 30 · $15.25 | 31 · $15.00 | 32 · $14.75 | 33 · $14.50 | 34 · $14.00 |
| Family | 35 · 1 × $38.00 | | | | |

Plan copy (public, from the same page): **Lean** — *"Built for cutting, dialing in & staying sharp"*,
250–450 cal, 25–35 g protein · **Signature** — *"Built for everyday health & performance"*, 450–650 cal,
35–60 g protein · **Performance** — *"Fuel for growth, strength & relentless output"*, 650–850 cal, 50–65 g
protein · **Family** — *"Healthy meals the whole family will actually eat"*, 3–4 servings.

Shape: every plan × count carries `mpid`, `meals_per_week`, `price_per_meal_cents`; the file carries
`read_on` and `read_from`. **The page shows 7 and 14 only**; the other counts are held because the price
ladder (*"buy more, save more"*) is a later rung. Money is integer cents; totals are computed, never stored.

## 3. The destination

- `https://fitafnutrition.com/order?mpid=<mpid>` — confirmed to open that plan's order page.
- The base URL is **one value in the data**, so the doors can be re-pointed (a future store) without
  touching the template.
- ⏳ **Rung 2 (not now)**: a pre-filled cart via a fragment the store's tag reads — unproven; until then
  links are the plain order URL.

## 4. Tests — `node --test`, no network

| | case | expect |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | `plans.json` | every plan × shown count has an integer `mpid` and integer cents; no two cells share an `mpid` |
| T2 | the built page | exactly 6 individual grid links + 1 family link, each `…/order?mpid=N`, and the set of N equals the table's |
| T3 | ⭐ Signature 14 | links to `mpid=29`, not 28 (the out-of-order pair) |
| T4 | weekly total | computed = meals × price for every cell (e.g. Lean 7 = $87.50) |
| T5 | no external requests | the built HTML has no `<script src>`, no `<link rel=stylesheet>` to another host, no `fetch(` |
| T6 | QR | each PNG decodes (or: the encoder's own round-trip) to exactly its event URL |
| T7 | ⭐ mutant | swap Signature 14/21 mpids in a copy of the data → T3 fails |
