# Why Fit AF?

Fresh, chef-prepared meals, made for your goals, delivered every week.

**Fresh, never frozen.** Every meal is cooked in our own kitchen in Scranton, Pennsylvania, for the week you eat it. We've been doing it since 2017.

**Built around your goals.** Lean for cutting and staying sharp, Signature for everyday health and performance, Performance for growth, strength and relentless output, and Family: healthy meals the whole family will actually eat.

**A new menu every week.** The favourites stay, the seasons come through, and there is always something new to try. Start from our Chef's Choice, or choose every meal yourself.

**Allergen sensitive.** Celiac, diabetic and other diets are fully accommodated.

**Delivered where you are.** Order by midnight Thursday and your meals arrive together on Sunday, at your home or your gym, across Greater Boston, Eastern Pennsylvania and parts of New Jersey, Delaware and New York. You don't need to be in.
