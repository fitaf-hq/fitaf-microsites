Why Fit AF?

Fresh, chef-prepared meals – made for your goals, delivered every week.

We cook all our meals from scratch in our own kitchen outside Boston. We've been delivering fresh meals (never frozen!) across the Northeast since 2017.

Questions

How does it work?

Set your goals and pick your menu – and we'll take it from there.

When do I order, and when do my meals arrive?

Each week's menu opens on Friday morning, and you can order until 11:59 PM Thursday. Your meals arrive together, in one bag, on Monday, at your home (you don't need to be there when they arrive).

Can I change a meal?

Yes! Our menus are just a starting point – swap any meal you like. If you need help or have a complex request, please contact us.

Can I skip a week, pause or cancel?

Yes! You can easily do this yourself – or our customer service team can handle it for you.
