Why Fit AF?

Fresh, chef-prepared meals, made for your goals, delivered every week.

Fit AF is a fresh meal delivery company from Northeast Pennsylvania. We cook in our own kitchen in Scranton, and every week we deliver fresh meals, never frozen, across Eastern Pennsylvania, parts of New Jersey, Northern Delaware, Southern New York and Greater Boston. We've been doing it since 2017.

What sets us apart

  • Fresh, never frozen. Your meals are cooked each week, for the week you eat them. You can't freeze a salad.
  • Allergen sensitive. Celiac, diabetic and other diets are fully accommodated. How a customer tells us about an allergy or a diet.
  • Built around your goals. Convenience for some, fitness for others, weight loss or management for others. Choose the plan that fits:
    • Lean: built for cutting, dialing in and staying sharp (250–450 calories and 25–35 g of protein a meal)
    • Signature: built for everyday health and performance (450–650 calories, 35–60 g of protein)
    • Performance: fuel for growth, strength and relentless output (650–850 calories, 50–65 g of protein)
    • Family: healthy meals the whole family will actually eat (3 to 4 servings)
  • A new menu every week. The favourites stay, the seasons come through, and there is always something new to try.
  • Delivered where you are. To your home or your gym, and you don't need to be in.

Questions

How does it work?

Choose your plan: lunch or dinner or both, weekdays only or every day, and breakfast if you like. Then choose your meals from this week's menu, or start from our Chef's Choice and change anything you want before you pay.

When do I order, and when do my meals arrive?

Each week's menu opens on Friday morning, and you can order until midnight on Thursday. Your meals arrive together, in one bag, on Sunday, at your home or at your gym, so you can pick them up as part of your routine. You don't need to be home when they arrive.

Can I change a meal?

Reasonable swaps are welcome: no side of potatoes, a turkey patty instead of beef, no cilantro, not spicy.
